### Slide 1: Title & Executive Vision
* **Title:** FINRISK AI: Autonomous Multi-Modal Financial Risk Intelligence Engine
* **Subtitle:** Incorporating Module B (Strategic Portfolio Stress Testing) & 3D/AR Spatial Risk Corridors
* **Team:** Sarth Joshi & Team | VIT

---

### Slide 2: The Core Problem
* Financial risk is fragmented: Market risks live in Bloomberg terminals, operational risks in transaction DBs, and sentiment risks on Twitter/Reuters news wires.
* Legacy stress testing is static, batched, and lacks real-time multi-dimensional correlation.
* Executives need intuitive spatial & holographic risk perception rather than 50-tab spreadsheets.

---

### Slide 3: FINRISK AI Solution Overview
* **Ingestion:** Real-time synthesis of banking transactions, financial news (CNBC, Reuters, Guardian), PhraseBank annotations, and high-frequency equity tweets.
* **Module B:** Strategic Portfolio Stress Testing with Monte Carlo VaR (95%) and Expected Shortfall (CVaR).
* **Surveillance:** Statistical Z-score + velocity + NLP narrative fraud & anomaly detection.
* **Spatial Experience:** Three.js 3D Risk Globe with outward risk towers and WebXR AR Lens HUD.

---

### Slide 4: Module B Deep Dive (Strategic Portfolio Stress Testing)
* **Predefined Macro Crises:** Global Recession (-25%), Inflation Spike (-15%), Black Swan Crash (-40%), Geopolitical Crisis (-20%), Pandemic Shock (-30%).
* **Interactive Dynamic Allocation:** Real-time reweighting across Tech, Financials, Healthcare, Energy, and Utilities.
* **Monte Carlo Engine:** 10,000 geometric Brownian motion paths over 21-day horizon calculating tail losses.
* **Comparison Matrix:** One-click comparative resilience benchmarking across all macro crises simultaneously.

---

### Slide 5: 3D Geospatial Globe & AR Spatial Lens
* **Geodesic Mapping:** Real-time rendering of key global financial exchanges (NYSE, LSE, Frankfurt, NSE/BSE, HKEX, TSE).
* **3D Spikes:** Radial height & color glow indicate local sovereign debt / market risk exposure.
* **AR Risk Lens:** Optical pass-through HUD targeting specific assets with holographic risk diagnostics and automated hedging advice.

---

### Slide 6: Technical Architecture & Performance
* **Backend:** FastAPI + SQLAlchemy SQLite + Lexical Financial Sentiment Pipeline.
* **Frontend:** Vite + React 18 + Three.js + Recharts (Dark Glassmorphic UI).
* **Performance:** Sub-50ms API response time, 60 FPS 3D rendering, 100% locally executable.
* **Compliance:** Zero external cloud dependencies, fully under 50MB git size threshold.
